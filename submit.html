Submit Photos

Send us your photos

Got a good shot from one of our stargazing trips or on-campus events? Send it in and we’ll add it to the gallery on the home page. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to install.

Please check first. Only send photos you took yourself, or that you have permission to share. If somebody in a photo would rather it were not published, please leave that one out. We credit photos to whoever sends them, unless you ask us not to.

Choose your photos

Pick your photos below. Your browser shrinks each one and strips its metadata — including any location data — before anything is sent. Nothing leaves your device until you press the button at the end.

JPEG, PNG or HEIC, up to 8 at a time. Large photos are fine — they are shrunk before they are sent.

    What happens next

    Your photos are added to the gallery, usually within a couple of minutes. Please send only photos you are happy to see published, and only ones you took yourself or have permission to share.

    If you change your mind, or want a photo taken down later, email usydastronomy@gmail.com and it will be removed.
