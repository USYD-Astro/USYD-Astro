Submit Photos

Send us your photos!

Got a good shot from one of our stargazing trips or on-campus events? Send it in and it joins the gallery below, which is where members’ photos live. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to install.

Photos are credited, never sold, and can be taken down again on request.

Sent in by members

Every photo here was sent in by a member. Select one to see it full size, along with who sent it and anything they told us about it.

What happens next

Your photos are added to the gallery on this page, usually within a few minutes, with your name and any details you gave us. The home page gallery is a curated selection, so photos sent in here do not appear there.

If you change your mind, or want a photo taken down later, email usydastronomy@gmail.com and it will be removed.
